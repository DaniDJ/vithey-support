Quick entry

An amount, a category: done.

Type the amount in the local currency and watch the conversion appear live. Pick a category and the expense is saved, with an Undo message in case you slip.

  • Every currency, at the day's rate.
  • Offline, the expense is saved and converted as soon as the network is back.
  • An expense can span several days: a hotel night, a rental.
  • Photograph a receipt: it stays in the app, never in your photo library.

Map and places

Your trip, as you lived it.

With your permission, Muskadia can geolocate your expenses. You get the most accurate map of your trip: not only where you spent money, but also a sense of your route and all the places you visited.

  • Add a place to each expense: your position, a recent place, or a typed name.
  • Nearby expenses are grouped by place, and the trip route links the stops.
  • Your position is only read when you ask for it. If you decline, just type the place name.

Frequently asked questions

What people often ask.

What is Muskadia?

An iPhone app to log travel expenses in the local currency and track the everyday budget, in a space called the Home. It was called Vithey until 22 October 2026.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no server receiving your expenses: everything is stored on your iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Without a network, the expense is saved and converted as soon as the connection returns.

Can we split expenses as a couple?

Yes. For each expense you say who paid and each person's share, and Muskadia works out who owes whom.

Does Muskadia use my location?

Only with your permission, and only when you ask for it on an expense. It places the expense on the map and draws your trip's route. If you decline, just type the place name: the app stays fully usable.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Your expenses stay on your phone. The app only downloads a list of exchange rates, without sending any of your data. There are no ads and no tracking tools.

Is it free?

Your first trip is free with all its features, and the Home is free. To open more trips, there is a one-time purchase, no subscription. The price is the one shown on the App Store.

Which languages?

French, English, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and German.

I used Vithey: do I keep my trips?

Yes. Vithey became Muskadia: it is the same app, and your trips are carried over.

How do I get my data out?

In Settings, export produces a spreadsheet (CSV) and the full backup can be restored in one tap.
